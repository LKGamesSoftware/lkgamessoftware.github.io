The game

Pong, with a competitive edge

Easy to learn in seconds, deep enough to master. Every match is a head-to-head duel against a real opponent.

1v1 online duels

Quick Match finds an opponent for you, or play a friend with a private code. Rollback netcode helps keep matches smooth on mobile connections.

Ranked and casual

Climb from Bronze to Master with an ELO-based rating, and see where you stand on the global leaderboard.

Perks that change the game

Power Shot, Long Paddle, Aimed Angle, Slow Field, Goal Shield, Move Boost and Ball Echo: build a loadout that suits your style.

Neon look, your colors

Unlock paddle colors, field themes and avatars. You see your opponent's choices too, so every match looks like it belongs to both players.

Built for phones

Designed for touch screens and wide displays, with a clean neon interface.

12 languages

English, Italian, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Korean, Japanese and Russian.

The studio
